Box Flow physics sandbox

How to play

  1. Pick a tool — belt, tube or box — from the top bar.
  2. Click the stage to place it. Belts start switched off — click them to run.
  3. Tubes: click and drag to draw the whole pipe path in one gesture, then release. Click the last tile and drag again to extend it.
  4. Select what you placed and use the floating controls: belts get − ▶ ◀ +, tubes get direction arrows you can hold to grow their path.
  5. Drop boxes with the box tool and chain belts & tubes into a little factory.
  6. Drag anything to rearrange. Right-click (or the trash tool) removes things.

Shortcuts: V select · B belt · T tube · X box · D delete · Esc done · M sound · Del remove selected. Everything autosaves in this browser; use Export / Import to share layouts.

Clear the whole factory?

All belts, tubes and boxes will be removed. This can't be undone.
